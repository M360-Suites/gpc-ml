Our Beginning

Built from the ground up,
for the long haul.

On the 9th of November 2009, GPC Energy and Logistics Limited was incorporated with a primary objective of conducting business in both the Oil & Gas and Logistics Sectors.

Over time, the desire of our founders to facilitate the circular flow of economic activity globally has seen the business focus primarily on professional logistics services. We have built a reputation in the industry for the delivery of our clients' goods "on time and in the right condition."

Today, we serve an array of clients across the FMCG, Construction, Manufacturing, and Agricultural sectors — relationships built on value, trust, and consistent service delivery.
