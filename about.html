About GPC

The Story Behind Every Mile

From a bold vision in 2009 to Nigeria's leading precision logistics partner — discover the people, values, and purpose that drive us forward.

Our Beginning

Built from the ground up,
for the long haul.

On the 9th of November 2009, GPC Energy and Logistics Limited was incorporated with a primary objective of conducting business in both the Oil & Gas and Logistics Sectors respectively.

Over time, the desire of our founders to facilitate the circular flow of economic activity globally has seen the business focus primarily on professional logistics services. We have built a reputation in the industry for the provision for the delivery of our clients' goods “on time and in the right condition”.

Today, we have an array of clients in the FMCG, Construction, Manufacturing, and Agricultural sectors with whom we have fostered long-term relationships based on value and service delivery.

To enable our value proposition, we operate out of 15 locations in Nigeria which are supported by 2 fully equipped maintenance facilities and truck parks.

Driven by our people, our values, and our overriding passion for excellence, GPC Energy and Logistics Limited has experienced unprecedented levels of success. We are strategically positioned to continuously transform haulage and logistics services in Nigeria and beyond, with a strong commitment to responsible and sustainable practices.

Our management team comprises seasoned professionals and entrepreneurs who have acquired specialist knowledge and expertise in transportation, supply chain management, oil and gas, finance, and marketing. We have leveraged the most modern I.T tools and software which deepens our intelligence capability and responsiveness.

As a company, we are strong, balanced, and believe in the strength of ideas, technical excellence, and financial prudence. We are empowered by an unwavering resilience, collective team spirit, and firm belief that nothing is impossible — that defines the culture of the GPC Energy and Logistics Limited workforce.
